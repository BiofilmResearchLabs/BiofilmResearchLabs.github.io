Research Story

Bacterial-fungal communities move as collectives

Microbes are often studied one cell at a time. In the mouth, however, they frequently encounter each other before reaching a tooth surface.

BRL researchers found that bacteria and fungi from human saliva can assemble into groups that move together across surfaces. The fungi provide a physical scaffold while attached bacteria remain part of the moving community, producing a form of collective behavior with consequences for colonization and disease.

Source: PubMed / Science Advances
