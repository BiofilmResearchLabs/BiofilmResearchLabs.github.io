Research Story

Nanozyme microcapsules reach confined infection sites

Some infections are difficult to treat not because antimicrobial agents are unavailable, but because the infected space is hard to reach.

This work combined magnetic navigation with catalytic nanozymes inside microcapsules. The resulting system could move through confined and branched environments while carrying antimicrobial activity to the target site.

Source: PubMed / Advanced Healthcare Materials
